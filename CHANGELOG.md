## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Import window listing Comu recordings, with New, Imported, Update available and Not processed yet badges.
- Notes with frontmatter, a link back to Comu, the summary, insights, action items as tasks, a chapter list and the transcript with speakers and timestamps.
- Optional audio download into the note's `-assets` folder.
- Sign in from the settings: a sign-in window that picks up the token by itself, or paste the token from the clipboard (for Google or Apple accounts). Shows who is signed in and when the token expires; Sign out.
- Settings: Test connection, output folder, subfolder and note name templates, duplicate handling, transcript layout, trashed recordings.
